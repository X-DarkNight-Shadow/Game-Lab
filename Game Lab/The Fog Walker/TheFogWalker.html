The Fog Walker


Lore

Origin — No one knows where it came from. Old trails and children’s whispers point to something that watches, then melts back into fog.

Behavior — It appears at dusk or in heavy mist. It stands still and looks long. When approached, it steps back and vanishes between trunks.

Sightings — People report seeing it at a distance, often with two glowing antennae. A few say it carries a small blade; none were close enough to prove it.
